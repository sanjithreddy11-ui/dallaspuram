# Dallaspuram — Telugu Kitchen & Bar

A complete digital restaurant platform built for **Dallaspuram – Telugu Kitchen & Bar**, combining the restaurant's brand identity, customer experience, online food ordering, table reservations, delivery integration, and restaurant information into a single platform.

This project goes beyond a conventional restaurant website. It acts as the restaurant's **digital storefront and customer interaction platform**, allowing customers to discover the restaurant, explore its story and menu, place orders from home, reserve tables, view the restaurant gallery, and access its location and contact information.

## 📌 About the Project

Dallaspuram is designed as a modern digital identity for a Telugu restaurant.

The objective was to build a platform where customers don't have to rely on separate applications to understand the restaurant, browse its menu, order food, or make a reservation.

The website brings these experiences together:

- Restaurant branding and identity
- Restaurant story and information
- Digital menu
- Online food ordering
- Home delivery
- Table reservations
- Third-party delivery partner integration
- Customer authentication
- Restaurant gallery
- Location and directions
- Contact and operating information

The result is a **full customer-facing restaurant ecosystem** rather than a static informational website.

# ✨ Core Features

## 🍽️ Online Food Ordering

Customers can browse the restaurant's digital menu and order food directly through the website.

The ordering flow allows customers to:

- Browse available dishes
- View dish descriptions
- Add items to the cart
- Modify their order
- Review the cart before checkout
- Provide delivery details
- Place an order
- Proceed through the order workflow

The ordering experience is designed to feel like a dedicated food-ordering application while remaining part of the restaurant's own website.

---

## 🚚 Third-Party Delivery Partner Integration

One of the major parts of this project is the integration with **third-party delivery and logistics APIs**.

Instead of building and maintaining an independent delivery fleet, the platform can communicate with external delivery partners through APIs to handle the logistics side of customer orders.
